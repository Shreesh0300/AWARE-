SS Foreword x

Ayurveda is the oldest system of Indian medicine. The awareness and demand of this
ancient life science (Ayurveda) is practiced across the country and its principles and
practices emphasize on prevention of diseases and promotion of positive health.

Ayurveda medical system is very effective in prevention & treatment of non -
communicable diseases and life style disorders. There are three types of treatment
modalities in Ayurveda viz shodhana(Panchakarma) therapy, shamana(Palliative)
therapy and nidana parivajana (avoidance of causative factors of the disease) described
by ancient Acharyas in Ayurvedic Classical literature in order to purify the body and
mind, cure the diseases and prevent the diseases, respectively.

ESI Corporation is giving due importance to promote AYUSH systems of medicine in its
hospitals and dispensaries for the benefit of IPs and their family members. AYUSH policy
in ESIC designed in 2015 also promises to expand AYUSH facilities in ES! Hospitals and
dispensaries as per the preference of local population. At present, AYUSH services are
being provided in 293 units across the country and in house panchakarma treatment
facilities are being developed in ES! hospitals.

The Ministry of AYUSH, Govt.of India has decided to celebrate ‘Lord Dhanwantari Jayanti’
as ‘National Ayurveda Day’ every year since 2016. This year, the theme of the day is “Pain
management through Ayurveda”. ESIC is also celebrating this day in all ESI hospitals and
dispensaries through medical camps and awareness activities.

The AYUSH division of ES! Corporation has compiled a book entitled “Ayurveda offering -
Herbal healing” with the details of vernacular names, therapeutic uses of medicinal
plants to help the common public and Ayurveda fraternity. | congratulate the efforts of
the AYUSH division in bringing this valuable book to popularize the AYUSH system

through ESI dispensaries & hospitals.

Ayurveda is time tested traditional system of health offering the physical, mental,
sensorial, spiritual, social and environmental well being and deals with preventive as well
as curative aspects medicine.

The principles of dinacharya (daily regimen), ritucharya (seasonal regimen) sadvirtta
(positive behavioral regimen) are the mantras for prevention of disease, while the drugs,
diet, do’s and don'ts are the essentials for treatment of disease.

The drugs containing single or multiple ingredients are used in Ayurveda for the
treatment of different diseases based on the need as well as condition of the patient.
Most of the drugs are herbal in origin, used in general practice while minerals and metals
are prescribed in special conditions with at most care under the Ayurvedic expertise
medical supervision. This book describes only herbal remedies for common maladies,
which are very safe and effective, meeting the interest of common public. Simple, single,
and selective combinations, which are easily available in the Indian kitchen, gardens and
local market, are included in this book making it highly appreciable.

The plant figures with useful parts and vernacular names in this book are highly useful

Awareness of Ayush Systems in India is increasing day by day. Earlier, all the Indian
Systems of Medicine come under one group i.e. ISM, which was under the Ministry of
Health and Family Welfare. Later, in 2014, all these Indian Systems of Medicines have
been renamed as AYUSH (Ayurveda, Yoga, Unani, Siddha and Homeopathy) under the
separate Ministry i.e. as Ministry of Ayush, Government of India.

Ayurveda System has started in ESIC in 1977 on pilot basis to meet the demand of IPs and
their family members in Delhi in two ESIC Dispensaries. Later on looking into the
importance and acceptance, more units are opened in various hospitals and
dispensaries. With the awareness of traditional medical systems in 2003, the
Homeopathy and Yoga systems are also started in ESIC. Presently, we have 154 Ayurveda
Units, 77 Homeopathy Units, 32 Yoga Units, 27 Siddha Units, 03 Unani Units in total 293
units present in various ESIC and ESIS Hospitals and Dispensaries across the India. In
addition, at present there are 16 Pancha karma units and 4 Kshra sutra units in ESIC and
ESIS.

The Ayush Division of ESIC Head Quarters celebrates International Day of Yoga on 21st
June every year and National Ayurveda Day on the day of Dhanteras every year. During
first National Ayurveda Day AYUSH division has released a book entitled Essential
Ayurvedic Drug list of ESIC. This year also, third international Yoga Day was celebrated by
conducting the yoga competitions and yoga lession among staff of Head Quarters on
21.06.2017 as a part of awareness and promotion or Ayush services.

It is my pleasure to release the book “Ayureda Offering Herbal Healing on the occasion of
2nd National Ayurveda Day at Headquarters Office, ESI Corporation, New Delhi.

Ayurveda, the traditional knowledge of life, is transmitted through a system called Guru
Sishya Parampara (Teacher taught system). The knowledge and experience of Gurus
was recorded by their intelligent disciples in the form of compendium (Samhita). The
Ayurvedic classical texts Charaka Samhita, Susruta Samhita and Kasyapa Samhita are the
best examples, which are the compilation of observations of thousands of years and time
tested experiences.

In ancient times, the Ayurvedic Physicians themselves used to collect the raw materials
and prepare the drugs for their routine practice. The Physicians were well aware of the
identification, selection, collection, processing, and preparation of the drug for a
particular disease, based on the constitution of the patient. Presently, this practice is
obsolete due to industrialization, modernization of the technology, urbanization and
also some environmental factors. Most of Physicians now depend upon the readily
available marketed preparations in their clinical practice. However, there is a need of
recollection, identification features, processing techniques of herbs, which are
commonly used, in Ayurvedic clinical practice, hence this book entitled “Ayurveda
Offering Herbal Healing” is prepared in easy, understandable, and systematic manner.

This book has details of more than hundred herbs, which are easily available and are
commonly used in routine ailments by the Ayurvedic practitioners apart from the
traditional practitioners from generations to generations. The plants and their useful
parts vernacular names, botanical names and therapeutic uses are described in this
book.
